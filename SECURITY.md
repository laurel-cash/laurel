# Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@laurel.cash**. Please don't open a public issue for it.

Say what you found, how to reproduce it, and which version (Settings > About)
or commit. Laurel is testnet-only today, so no real funds are at stake, but a
flaw that could lose money on mainnet matters just as much now.

Laurel's threat model, its non-custodial rule and the money-loss audit are in
[docs/SECURITY.md](docs/SECURITY.md) and
[docs/SECURITY-TODO.md](docs/SECURITY-TODO.md).
